# Indonesian: 1-year study plan

The plan the Indonesian pages ([indonesian.md](indonesian.md)) follow: casual Indonesian for VTubers, creators,
chat and social media, about 5 h a week of focused study, English as the only support language, starting from a
rusty beginner. The build script turns this into `data/plan.json`, so Today knows the week's lesson and the
Log page knows each month's targets.

## The goal for month 12

- **Read** casual social media (posts, comments, stream titles, chat) understanding most of it, looking up roughly
  one word per post or less.
- **Write** chat messages and short posts naturally, with casual forms and particles (*udah, gak, sih, dong, wkwk*).
- **Follow** casual streams and videos: the gist of solo chatting streams without subtitles, details with them. Fast
  multi-person collabs will still be hard; that's normal at this stage.
- **Vocabulary**: about 2,600 words recognised, about 1,000 of them usable when writing.

Rough cost: 52 weeks × 5 h ≈ 260 h of study, plus whatever immersion you add by watching and reading for fun.
That's a strong casual-reading level (around B1 for reading and writing), not full fluency, and realistic for the time.

## A study week

| Day | Time | What |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monday | 60 min | Session, with **this week's lesson** in the middle block |
| Tuesday | 60 min | Session, with **drills** (numbers, time, prices) |
| Wednesday | 60 min | Session, with **affixes** (from month 4; until then, the lesson again) |
| Thursday | 60 min | Session, with **longer immersion and mining** |
| Friday | 60 min | Session, with **longer writing** and a 5-minute look at the week on the Log page |
| Saturday, Sunday | 10–15 min | **Reviews only.** Watch or read for fun if you like; log it as immersion |

About 5.3 h a week. Days can move around; what matters is five study days and reviews every day.

### One 60-minute session

| Minutes | Block | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–15 | Reviews | Everything due. The Review page stops at 15 min and carries the rest to the next block if needed |
| 15–25 | New words | 10 new cards from the deck, each with its example sentence and audio |
| 25–40 | Day's focus | Lesson, drills, affixes, as in the week above |
| 40–55 | Immersion | Months 1–3: song lyrics and short clips with subtitles. From month 4: a clip, thread or stream segment, mining 3–5 sentences |
| 55–60 | Writing | 2–5 casual sentences on the Log page: what you did, what you watched, a reply you'd post |

## New words: the pace

- **10 new cards per study day**, none at weekends: 50 a week, about 215 a month, about 2,600 in the year.
- New words come in `deck.json` order (most frequent in casual speech and online text first), plus anything you mine.
  Mined words count towards the 10.
- **Recall** (English → Indonesian, typed) is turned on for the first 800 deck words and for words you mine, about
  1,000 by month 12. The rest are recognition only, which is what reading needs.
- Rough coverage: the most common 1,000 words make up around 80% of casual speech, 2,500 around 90%. The remaining
  gap is names, slang and topic words, which mining fills.

## The year, month by month

The **words** column is the deck total at the end of the month. Grammar comes as one lesson a week; the month's
lessons are listed together.

## Checkpoints

The Log page lists each month's checkpoint with a score box (understood %, lookups, drill accuracy, or done/not
done) and a note. They're for adjusting the plan, not passing or failing.

## Adjusting as you go

| If | Then |
| --- | --- |
| Reviews take more than 25 minutes on study days for a week | Lower new cards to 6 a day until reviews are back under 20 minutes |
| Retention drops below 85% | Fewer new cards, and look at leeches: rewrite unclear cards, add an example, or suspend them |
| A card has failed 6 or more times | It's flagged as a leech on the Words page to rewrite or split |
| Days were missed | Never catch up on new cards. Do reviews first (at most 200 a day; the rest are spread over the next days) |
| It's too easy | Raise new cards to 12–15 a day, or move the next month's lessons forward |
| Holidays or a busy week | Weekend mode every day: reviews only |
| You fall behind the words column | Fine. The columns are a guide; the month's grammar still moves on, and the deck order keeps the useful words first |

## What the pages track for this

- **Today**: the week's lesson, the day's focus from the week template, new-card and review counts, minutes so far.
- **Calendar/Today tasks**: *Indonesian reviews* (ticked when nothing is due) and *Indonesian study* (four
  15-minute blocks).
- **Log**: study hours per week against 5 h, immersion hours, words per month against the plan's column, retention,
  checkpoints.
